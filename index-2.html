About Yakubu Nana

Yakubu Nana is a Nigerian geologist with hands-on experience in geophysical surveys, geological field mapping, borehole drilling and groundwater quality assessment. She holds a B.Sc. in Geology (Second Class Upper) from Federal University Gusau and is HSE certified (Levels 1 to 3 and IECHSE).

She works with ArcGIS, QGIS, WinRESIST and IP2WIN, uses GPS for site referencing and navigation, and has led field teams, interpreted geophysical data and written technical reports. She speaks English and Hausa.

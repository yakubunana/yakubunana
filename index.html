About Yakubu Nana

Yakubu Nana is a geologist based in Zaria, Nigeria. She holds a BSc in Geology (Second Class Upper) from Federal University Gusau and has completed the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

Her work focuses on finding and assessing groundwater: running geophysical surveys, siting and supervising boreholes, and analysing water quality. She is certified in Health, Safety and Environment (HSE Levels 1 to 3) and is a member of NAPE-YP and NMGS-YP. She speaks English and Hausa fluently.
